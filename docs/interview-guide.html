Hospital Workflow — Interview Guide

HospiCare · Hospital Management System (EMR & Pharmacy)

Prepared to map the real patient-care process before finalising the system.

Internship Project
How to use this guide. Take it to each member of staff and ask the questions in their section. Write answers on the dotted lines (or a notebook). The goal is to learn who does what, in what order, and the clinical rules (which tests follow which symptoms, and which drugs treat which diagnoses). The last two pages have tables to capture those rules — they will power the system's test/treatment suggestions.

1. Receptionist / Records Officer — first point of contact

  1. What details do you record for a new patient? (name, date of birth, sex, phone, address, next of kin, blood group, known allergies?)
  2. How do you find a returning patient's file? Is there a patient card or number?
  3. Do you record the reason for the visit, or does the nurse/doctor do that?
  4. Is any payment collected at registration (e.g. a consultation fee)? How much, and how is it recorded?
  5. After registration, where does the patient go next — to a nurse, straight to the doctor, or to a waiting area?
  6. How are appointments handled today (walk-in only, appointment book, phone)?

2. Nurse — triage & vital signs (key interview)

  1. After registration, does the patient see you before the doctor?
  2. Which vital signs do you measure? (temperature, blood pressure, pulse, weight, height, respiratory rate, oxygen saturation?)
  3. Do you record the patient's symptoms / complaint, and in how much detail?
  4. Do you decide how urgent a patient is (triage)? What categories do you use?
  5. Can you order any tests yourself, or only the doctor?
  6. Do you give any treatment before the doctor (injections, first aid, first dose)?
  7. Are there danger signs that mean a patient must be seen immediately?

Common complaint → tests usually done (the nurse often knows these best)

For each complaint, ask: "which lab tests are almost always ordered?"

  1. Fever / hot body → …
  2. Headache → …
  3. Cough / difficulty breathing → …
  4. Diarrhoea & vomiting → …
  5. Abdominal / stomach pain → …
  6. Fatigue / weakness / pallor → …
  7. Pregnancy-related visit → …

3. Doctor — consultation & clinical decisions

  1. What do you review first when the patient comes in (vitals, history, previous visits)?
  2. How do you decide which lab tests to order? Are there standard "symptom → test" rules you follow?
  3. After results come back, does the patient return to you, and how do you receive the results?
  4. How do you reach a diagnosis and choose drugs?
  5. For the most common diagnoses, what are the first-line drugs and typical dosing? (see table on last page)
  6. When do you write a referral to another facility?
  7. Would a system that suggests tests/drugs from the patient's symptoms help you? What would make you trust it (or not)?

4. Laboratory Technician — tests & results

  1. Which tests do you perform in-house, and which are sent out?
  2. How do you receive a test request (paper form, verbal, patient brings a slip)? What is written on it?
  3. How are results recorded and returned to the doctor?
  4. What is the typical turnaround time for common tests?
  5. How do you know a result is abnormal (reference ranges)? Do you flag it?
  6. What are the prices of the common tests? (see table)

5. Pharmacist — dispensing & stock

  1. How do you receive a prescription (paper, patient brings it, electronic)?
  2. Do you dispense exactly as prescribed, or can you substitute a generic/alternative?
  3. How is drug stock tracked now? When and how do you reorder?
  4. How do you handle expiry — how are near-expiry or expired drugs managed?
  5. Is payment made at the pharmacy or at a separate cashier?
  6. Do you sell to walk-in customers without a prescription (over-the-counter)?
  7. Which drugs are always in demand?

6. Cashier / Billing — if this is a separate person

  1. What is charged, and at which points (registration, consultation, lab, drugs)?
  2. Which payment methods do you accept? (cash, MTN Mobile Money, Orange Money, other?)
  3. Is there any insurance, exemption, or credit arrangement?
  4. How is a receipt issued and recorded?

7. Administrator / Management — oversight

  1. What reports do you need (daily revenue, patient numbers, stock levels, staff activity)?
  2. Who should be able to see or change what (roles & permissions)?
  3. What are the biggest pain points with the current (paper?) system?
  4. How is patient confidentiality handled?
